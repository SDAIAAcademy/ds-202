# Source note: the open district table

- **Publisher:** the Open Data Portal of Madinat Al-Sidr Municipality (a fictional publisher, for this course).
- **Dataset:** "Population and households by district, 2022", file `district_population_2022.csv`, published 2023-03-15, downloaded 2026-10-02.
- **Owner:** the statistics office of the municipality. Nobody at Ghilal owns it; Rimas Al-Sinani keeps the downloaded copy.
- **What one row is:** one district: population, households, and an estimate of low-income households from the 2022 household survey.
- **Coverage:** the 28 districts that existed in 2022. Districts D29 and D30 were established in 2024 and are not in the table.
- **Basis for use:** open data under the open licence of the portal: free reuse with attribution to the publisher.
- **Classification:** Public.
- **Known limits:**
  - The reference year is 2022; the city has grown since, unevenly.
  - `low_income_households_est` is a survey estimate; the portal publishes no margin of error for districts.
  - "Low income" in the survey is not the eligibility rule of the network.
